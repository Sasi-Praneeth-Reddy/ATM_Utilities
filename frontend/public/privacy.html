ATM Hub — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

ATM Hub is an internal scheduling and operations tool used by Around Town Movers (Hometown Services LLC). It is used only by the company's own staff and is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the company's authorization, the app connects to one company Google (Gmail) mailbox to read automated business report emails and their attachments, such as SmartMoving Smart Insights reports and Job Materials reports. It also works with the company's own job, customer and scheduling data from its SmartMoving CRM account.

How the data is used

Data is used only to keep the company's internal schedule, job board and task lists up to date. The app does not use Gmail data for advertising, and does not sell, rent or share it with third parties. Data is processed and stored in the company's own cloud accounts (Google Firebase and Amazon Web Services) and is accessible only to authorized company staff.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Data is kept for as long as it is needed for company operations. Access for the Google account can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings, and stored data can be deleted on request.
